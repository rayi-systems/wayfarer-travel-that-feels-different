How we travel

Built for people who want the trip, not the itinerary

We design every journey around what you'd actually want to remember in ten years — not the schedule of a sightseeing bus. Small groups (max 10), local guides, and enough free time to lose track of the afternoon.

Local guides only

Every trip is led by someone who actually lives there — often a chef, a sailor, or a former park ranger.

Small groups, max 10

A minibus of 40 is not a journey. Our vans seat ten, our tables seat ten, our days move at ten-person pace.

Carbon-balanced

Every booking offsets 2.4× its flights and contributes to verified local conservation projects.

Independent stays

Family-run riads, restored farmhouses, a night in a lighthouse. No two consecutive hotels look the same.
